{% trans "Talking to the rabbit (speech recognition)" %}
{% trans "Native voice commands are available only in French. Hold the button, wait for the beep, speak, then release. Recording stops after 15 seconds." %}

{% trans "The native vocabulary contains 28 phrases for ten actions. Say one phrase per button press. Free-form requests, extra first names and other historical variants are not supported." %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Clock: Quelle heure est-il ? or Donne-moi l'heure.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Weather: Météo, La météo, Donne-moi la météo, Quel temps fait-il ?, La météo aujourd'hui or La météo demain.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Air quality: Qualité de l'air.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Tai chi: Tai-chi or C'est l'heure du tai-chi.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Sleep: Dodo, Au lit or Dormir.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Mood and surprises: Comment vas-tu ?, Comment ça va ?, Donne-moi ton humeur, Fais-moi rire or Guili guili.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Magic 8 Ball: Dis lapin, T'en penses quoi Nabaztag ? or Tu en penses quoi Nabaztag ?.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Carrot: Carotte or Tu veux une carotte.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Rabbit presentation: Que sais-tu faire ? or Tu sais faire quoi ?.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Birthday: Anniversaire or C'est mon anniversaire.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Weather announces today or tomorrow, without an hourly forecast. Air quality reports today. Birthday replies are generic. Open conversation uses the separate Nabaztag IA application on the Mac." %}

{% trans "As a last resort, you can also shutdown the rabbit by pushing its head button quickly three times in a row." %}

{% trans "Base LED pulse color" %} {% trans "indicates the rabbit's network connection state" %}
{% blocktrans %}Fuchsia means all is fine: rabbit has an IP address on your local network, and access to the Internet.
Orange means not so good: rabbit has a local IP address, but no Internet connection (check your router).
Red means real bad: rabbit has no IP address (check your WiFi access point / DHCP server).{% endblocktrans %}
